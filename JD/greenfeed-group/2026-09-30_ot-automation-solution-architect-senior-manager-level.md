<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p><p>GREENFEED is an integrated Feed - Farm - Food - Logistics group operating manufacturing plants, farms and other operational sites. We are looking for an OT &amp; Automation Solution Architect to define and govern scalable, secure and standardized OT (Operational Technology) and automation solutions integrated with enterprise IT, data and cloud platforms.</p><p>The role works across SCADA, PLC, industrial networks, edge/IoT and automation environments, partnering with Operations, Engineering, Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Data &amp; AI and technology partners.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong> Key Responsibilities</strong></p><p>1. OT Architecture &amp; Standards</p><p><strong>• </strong>Own target architecture, reference designs, technology roadmaps and technical standards for OT and automation across plants, farms and other operational sites.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Lead architecture reviews and provide technical direction for new initiatives, plant upgrades and site expansion projects.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Drive reusable and scalable designs while balancing site-specific operational requirements; evaluate technology options based on reliability, lifecycle, interoperability, security and total cost of ownership.</p><p><br/></p><p>2. SCADA, Automation &amp; Industrial Connectivity</p><p><strong>• </strong>Define architecture principles and standards for SCADA, PLC, HMI, historian, sensors, gateways and related automation platforms.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Support modernization and integration of existing control environments with minimal production disruption.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Define industrial network and connectivity requirements, including segmentation, redundancy, secure remote access and site-to-enterprise connectivity; support plant and farm automation initiatives that improve visibility, control, productivity and equipment reliability.</p><p><br/></p><p>3. OT Integration, Data &amp; Cloud</p><p><strong>• </strong>Define integration architecture between OT systems and enterprise applications, data platforms and cloud services.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Establish standard OT data exchange patterns using technologies such as OPC UA, MQTT, APIs, gateways or messaging.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Enable secure use of operational data for monitoring, analytics, optimization and AI; work with Data &amp; AI and application teams to reduce unnecessary point-to-point integrations and improve data reliability and traceability.</p><p><br/></p><p>4. OT Security &amp; Resilience</p><p><strong>• </strong>Work with Cybersecurity and Infrastructure teams to define OT security architecture and controls, including segmentation, secure remote access, privileged access, logging, backup and recovery.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Support OT security assessments and remediation while considering operational continuity and safety.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Ensure appropriate resilience, redundancy and recovery capabilities for critical operations.</p><p><br/></p><p>5. Architecture Governance &amp; Vendor Management</p><p><strong>• </strong>Own architecture governance, technical requirements, design guidelines and solution review criteria for OT and automation initiatives.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Review and challenge vendor designs and proposals; participate in technical evaluations, proof-of-concepts and vendor selection.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Reduce unnecessary technology fragmentation and vendor dependency through standardization, documentation and effective knowledge transfer.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong> Qualifications &amp; Experience</strong></p><p><strong>• </strong>Bachelor's degree in Automation, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Control Engineering, Information Technology or a related field.</p><p><strong>• </strong>7+ years of relevant experience in industrial automation, OT, manufacturing technology or related environments, including solution design or architecture experience.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Strong practical knowledge of SCADA, PLC, HMI, industrial networking and automation architectures, with experience connecting OT systems to enterprise IT, cloud or data platforms.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Good understanding of industrial communication protocols, OT cybersecurity, network segmentation, secure remote access, backup and recovery.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Experience working with manufacturing plants, farms, industrial facilities or other distributed operational environments.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Ability to translate operational requirements into practical, scalable and maintainable technology solutions, with strong analytical, communication, stakeholder and vendor management skills.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Experience with Siemens, Schneider Electric or equivalent automation platforms; familiarity with OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, historian, IoT/edge computing or predictive maintenance is an advantage.</p><p><strong>• </strong>Knowledge of ISA-95, Purdue Model, IEC 62443 or similar OT architecture/security frameworks, relevant certifications, and experience in food, feed, agriculture, livestock or process industries are an advantage.</p>
